**Figure Q1.** *Effect of damping parameter ($\lambda$) on informational structuring.*  
Representative public Tier-0/Tier-1 regeneration showing $\rho(x,t)$ under a qualitative
$\lambda$ sweep. Lower damping yields longer-lived and more weakly damped structures
with persistent propagation across the $(x,t)$ domain, whereas higher damping produces
faster decay and shorter-lived structuring fronts. This figure is intended as a public-facing
qualitative sweep figure for Appendix Q.
